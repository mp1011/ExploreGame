# GOAL
Give the Light Spirit (LS) the ability to turn on light switches as it hunts the player while in the HalfPresence Phase

# CONTEXT
- LightSwitch class
	- GeometryBuilder\Shapes\Appliances\LightSwitch.cs
	- exposes an "On" property that the LS will manipulate
- HalfPresencePhaseHandler class 
	- Logics\Controllers\LightSpiritPhases\HalfPresencePhaseHandler.cs
	- controller for Light Spirit 


# BEHAVIOR
- look for any LightSwitch objects in the same Room as the LightSpirit
- if any are found that are off, change the CurrentTarget of its PathFinder to the switch
- when the LS is "close enough" to the switch (4 feet), it will turn the switch On and then reset the CurrentTarget back to the Player
