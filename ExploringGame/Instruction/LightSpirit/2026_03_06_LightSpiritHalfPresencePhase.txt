# SUMMARY
Implement the "Half Presence" Phase of the Light Spirit (aka LS) as described below.

# PHASE CHARACTERISTICS
- the LS is not visible, but it is physically present in the world
	- the LS collides with obstacles and the floor, it is treated as a normal solid object
- the LS will seek out the player
- the LS will turn on any light switch it comes across
- the LS will open doors if needed to leave a room
- lights will pulsate brighter when the LS is near

# CONTEXT
 - define this behavior in HalfPresencePhaseHandler, located at `Logics\Controllers\LightSpiritPhases\HalfPresencePhaseHandler.cs`
 - pathfinding module can be found at `Logics\Pathfinding\PathFinder.cs`
